emissions from each treatment unit.

organic matter in the anaerobic digestion stage, the production of CH4 can be minimized.

nitrification and denitrification tanks can regulate the metabolic activities of

and TN removal through correlation analysis, this analytical method has certain
limitations. Correlation analysis can only reflect the degree of association between
variables, is unable to reveal causal relationships, and has not quantified the relative
impacts of variables such as COD and TN removal on CH₄ and N₂O emissions. Future
research could consider using methods such as regression analysis or generalized additive
models (GAMs) to further explore the relationships among these variables.
