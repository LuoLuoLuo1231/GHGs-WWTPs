4. Discussion
We constructed a comprehensive GHG emission accounting system
for Shanghai’s municipal WWTPs and projected emissions trends up to
2050. Our results revealed that through the implementation of advanced
water conservation and technical improvement, the municipal WWTPs
in Shanghai could potentially cap GHG emissions at 0.55 Mt CO2-eq by
2050. Despite achieving a substantial reduction in GHG emissions,
further endeavors are imperative for the municipal WWTPs to attain
carbon neutrality. The reduction ratio in technical improvement we
