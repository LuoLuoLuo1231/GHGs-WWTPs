Original Research Article
Estimating nitrous oxide emissions
based on TN discharge from
municipal wastewater treatment
plants: A case study for the city of
Xiamen, China
Ruolin Bai,College of Environmental Science and Engineering, Xiamen University of Technology,
Xiamen, Fujian, China
Lei Jin and Haiyan Fu,Key Laboratory of Environmental Biotechnology (XMUT), Fujian Province
University, Fujian Engineering and Research Center of Rural Sewage Treatment and Water Safety,
Xiamen University of Technology, Xiamen, Fujian, China
Mazhan Zhuang,Xiamen Institute of Environmental Sciences, Xiamen, Fujian, China
Yi Wei,College of Environmental Science and Engineering, Xiamen University of Technology, Xiamen,
Fujian, China
Abstract: Mitigation of nitrous oxide (N O) emissions is of primary importance to meet the targets of
2
reducing carbon footprints of wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs). This paper takes the N O
2
discharged from a case study of wastewater treatment plants as the main research object, and then
developsanovelalgorithm,whichcanaccuratelyestimatetheamountofN Orelease,andthenapplies
2
it to the local wastewater treatment plants. According to the results, the nitrous oxide emission flux and
the emission factors (EFs) are discussed. The results include the following: (1) The total amount of N O
2
discharged from Xiamen wastewater treatment plants between 2018 and 2019 was 3881.29 kg and
3642.97kg,respectively;(2)theproductionofN Oemissionsbasedonthetotalnitrogen(TN)algorithm
2
in the existing wastewater treatment factories in Xiamen was obtained based on the research EFs; and
(3) by controlling other factors of WWTPs process such as chemical oxygen demand /nitrogen (COD)/
(N)ratio,dissolvedoxygen(DO)concentration,pHvalue(thedegreeofconcentrationofhydrogenions),
and solids retention time (SRT) in the wastewater treatment process, selecting a secondary wastewater
treatment process with an economical and reasonable approach can reduce N O emissions in the
2
wastewater treatment process. © 2023 Society of Chemical Industry and John Wiley & Sons, Ltd.
