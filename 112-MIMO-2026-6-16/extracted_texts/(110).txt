wastewater treatment, a primary source of NO and CH emissions. We have
2 4

facilities (WRRFs) that collectively treat 9% of US centralized wastewater.
Facility-level emissions were scaled to the national level using a probabilistic
approach. Here we show that the measured emissions were 1.9 times higher
for N O (95% confidence interval (CI): 1.3–2.6) and 2.4 times higher for CH

for mitigation. Although detected at only a small fraction of facilities,
measurements of NH emissions (86 kt yr−1 in the USA) suggest WRRFs are

wastewater treatment emissions is needed to reach sustainability targets.
