A R T I C L E I N F O A B S T R A C T
Keywords: Accurate accounting of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs) is critical to
Wastewater treatment plants achieving carbon reduction targets, optimizing operation and management, and promoting sustainable devel-
Greenhouse gases opment of the industry. Currently, the accurate accounting of GHG emissions from WWTPs primarily considers
Emission factors
the differences in processes and regions, but overlooks the impact of temporal variations on GHG emissions. In
Accurate accounting
this study, a long-term study was conducted in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region on WWTPs using the AAO
Seasonality
process. The characteristics of GHG emissions from WWTPs were analyzed, and GHG emissions from WWTPs in
different seasons were compared in multiple dimensions, emphasizing the seasonal differences in GHG emission
factor (EF) from WWTPs. The results showed that the direct emission of GHG increased significantly in summer,
and the mean EF-CH4 was maximized to 13.35±4.91 gCH4/kgCOD, which was 1.1–2.5 times higher than the EF
in other seasons. The mean EF-N2O was maximized at 4.17 gN2O/kgTN, which was 1.3–8.2 times higher than the
EF in other seasons. Therefore, the influence of seasonal differences on GHG emissions from WWTPs should not
be ignored, and it is of great significance to improve the accuracy of GHG emissions accounting in this industry.

This study selected four typical AAO-process WWTPs in the BTH
region to conduct a long-term investigation. In situ GHG emission
measurements were carried out in different seasons to characterize the
seasonal variations of GHG (CO2, CH4, N2O) emitted from the WWTPs.
The EF of GHG from WWTPs in the BTH region was calculated, the
changes of EF in different seasons were discussed, and it was proposed
that the changes of EF on seasonal scales should be taken into account in
the accounting of EF.
