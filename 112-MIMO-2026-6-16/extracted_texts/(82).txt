Editor: Rong Chen With the rapid development of liquor industry in Zunyi City, the discharge amount of liquor wastewater had
increased significantly, leading to severe challenge in wastewater treatment. Meanwhile, greenhouse gases
Keywords: (GHGs) emissions associated with wastewater treatment processes had gained increasing attention. In this study,
Benefits evaluation eight indicators related to operating costs and emission reduction benefits were weighted by entropy weight

Wastewater treatment plant
established to comprehensively assess the synergy benefits of contaminants and GHGs emission reduction of
liquor wastewater treatment plants (LWTPs). The assessment results indicated that half of the eight LWTPs
needed to be improved in terms of operating costs, sludge production, total investment, and GHGs emissions.
Further analysis revealed that the excessive use of chemical reagents such as sodium hydroxide, sulfuric acid, and
hydrogen peroxide had led to inefficient LWTPs higher costs and GHGs emissions. Additionally, by analyzing the
factors outside the assessment method, it was found that the current wastewater treatment technology was fully
capable of coping with the increase of influent TN or TP concentration, but could not cope with the increase of
water volume load rate or influent NH3-N concentration. Based on these findings, this study proposed some
targeted reconstruction suggestions, aiming to provide theoretical basis and practical references for the green
and low-carbon reconstruction of LWTPs.
